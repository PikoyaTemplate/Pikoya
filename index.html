Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Choose a service, select a plan or quantity, enter the required details, and add the item to your cart.

What happens after checkout?

The demo creates an order number and stores the order in your browser so you can review the front-end flow.

Can I customize the services?

Yes. Service names, plans, prices, copy, fields and visual styling are all editable in the source files.

Is this ready for a real business?

The template is front-end ready. Real authentication, payments, databases and fulfillment should be connected to a secure backend.
